## Recording notes
- Read it a few times, then say it in your own words. A natural delivery beats a word-perfect one.
- Frame from the chest up, with a light source in front of you, and use a quiet room. A phone on a tripod at eye level is fine.
- Cut to a screen recording of VaporFriends and Countie Live while you mention them. The VaporFriends promo (`SteamFriends/motion/out/`) works too.
- Upload it to YouTube (unlisted is fine) and paste the link into "Video introduction" on your Upwork profile.
